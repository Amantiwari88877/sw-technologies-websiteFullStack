Who We Are

From a Garage Idea to a Trusted Studio

SW Technologies was founded in 2022 with one clear goal: to help businesses win online. We started as a two-person freelance team working out of a small apartment in Noida — fuelled by coffee, code, and an obsession with clean design.

Today, we're a growing studio of developers, designers, and digital strategists who have delivered 50+ projects across India and beyond. Every website we build is hand-crafted with care — no templates, no shortcuts.

We believe the best websites don't just look good. They load fast, rank high, convert visitors, and grow with your business. That's the SW Technologies promise.
